🎯 Executive Summary

🔥 CRITICAL BREAKTHROUGH: Utilization vs Billable Capacity Resolved

The Core Problem: Matt wanted utilization to show "are we feeding our team 40 billable hours?" but textbook utilization measures billable÷(billable+non-billable).

The Solution: Create TWO separate metrics:

  1. Utilization Rate - Textbook definition (billable÷total hours)
  2. Billable Capacity Rate - Actual billable vs 40-hour target

Impact: Matt gets BOTH industry-standard metric AND his operational metric.

📅 Frequency: Weekly (Default: Last Week + MTD) | 🎯 Target: 89% Utilization

FORMULAS:
Utilization: Billable Hours ÷ (Billable + Non-billable) × 100
Capacity: Billable Hours ÷ 40 × 100 (measures if we hit 40 billable hours target)

KPI 6: Work Backlog Hours

📅 Frequency: Weekly | 🎯 Target: 3-6 weeks per person
⚠️ Alert: ≥8 weeks per person = Overload (Consider Hiring)
📊 Built in: Airtable | 🔗 Data Source: Monday.com (PM/drafter estimates per active job)
FORMULAS:
Total Backlog: Σ Estimated Hours (all active jobs)
Backlog per Staff: Total Backlog ÷ Team Size
Weeks per Staff: Backlog per Staff ÷ 40 hours
Alert Trigger: If Backlog per Staff ≥ 8 weeks (320 hours) → Consider hiring
Implementation Notes:
  • Uses current manual estimation by PMs/drafters per active job
  • Weekly trend tracking identifies capacity constraints early
  • Standardize estimation method over time for consistency
  • Hiring Decision Trigger: ≥8 weeks per person = overloaded, time to recruit
  • Healthy Range: 3-6 weeks per person maintains steady workflow
